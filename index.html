📲 How to Add Locket Widget

Step 1: Press & Hold Screen

Long press any empty area on your phone's home screen until the apps jiggle.

Step 2: Tap the (+) Add Button

Search for "Locket" in your widgets menu.

Step 3: Pick Size & Place

Choose Small (2x2) or Medium (4x2) and place it on your home screen.

Step 4: See Live Moments!

Your widget will now receive live photos whenever your friends snap one!
